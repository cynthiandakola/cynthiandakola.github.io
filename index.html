Motivation and interests

I am interested in how brands communicate clearly and consistently over time. I like seeing how small changes in wording, visuals, or timing can change the way people react.

To keep learning, I follow marketing newsletters, save examples of campaigns I like, and practise by creating my own small content pieces.

  • Curating a small swipe file of favorite campaigns.
  • Practising content ideas and rewrites for different brands.
  • Experimenting with posts and simple reports.

What I am like to work with

As a colleague, I am steady rather than loud. I like to prepare, ask clarifying questions, and then get on with the work. I am happiest when I can analyze a situation, support a team, keep things organized, and see progress over time. I have a highly analytical mind, and can make quick sense of data to inform my work. I enjoy turning insights into actionable strategies.

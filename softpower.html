Brand concept

The idea behind Soft Power Syndicate and what it aims to express.

Brand

Brand

Soft Power Syndicate is a hobby-brand series built around visual storytelling and mood-driven aesthetics. Each piece aims to evoke emotion and provoke thought through carefully chosen visuals and narratives centred on Black womanhood. The recurring theme, “What They Don’t Tell You About Being a Black Woman,” focuses on unspoken experiences, quiet strength, and everyday realities that are often overlooked.

Direction and rationale

Why this project, and how it connects to my background and interests.

Rationale

Soft Power Syndicate

I manage a digital storytelling platform focused on visuals that explore the experiences I face as a Black woman, especially those that are rarely given space in mainstream media. The work includes poetic slideshows such as “They never warn you that your peace will feel loud,” combining themes of Black excellence, emotional insight, and cultural subtleties.

This concept builds on my background in psychology and my interest in exploring emotions without revealing too much. I am drawn to Black futurism and editorial styles, so the project becomes a mix of creativity and strategy. It gives room to experiment with AI tools and artistic direction, focusing on layered storytelling rather than purely personal narratives.

Inspirational brands

References that shaped the visual direction, mood, and storytelling approach.

Inspiration
Inspirational Brand 1

EveryStylishGirl

  • Multimedia platform highlighting the achievements of Black women in fashion and media.
  • Mix of editorial images, personal stories, collaborations, and approachable captions.
  • Strong, uplifting visual style that showcases Black femininity in industry spaces.
  • Key learning: keep the feed cohesive and pair visuals with concise, empowering captions.

Brand 2

Black Women in Visual Art

  • Instagram account managed by Black women artists and curators, spotlighting visual work and events.
  • Uses calm, editorial photography with minimal text.
  • Treats the feed like a curated moodboard or museum wall.
  • Key learning: let imagery lead and use text sparingly to support the mood.

Brand 3

Llanakila

  • Visual artist focusing on colorful and lively portraits of Black women, with a touch of the futuristic, exploring themes of freedom and identity through striking imagery.
  • What they do well: Turn feelings into visual art; creates meaningful and stylish images.
  • What you can learn: How to use visuals to tell deeper stories; balancing mood, color, and message in art created with AI.
Marketing goal and channels

How Soft Power Syndicate will show up online in its first phase.

Goal

Launch goal

  • Launch an Instagram account with nine thoughtfully curated posts.
  • Create at least five visually strong, mood-focused pieces using AI, shared as carousels or short reels.
  • Repurpose at least one visual story on Pinterest to broaden reach.
  • Use a mix of three AI tools (for example ChatGPT for captions, an image model for visuals, and a TTS tool for voiceovers).
Persona

A mood-based audience profile for Soft Power Syndicate.

Audience

Mood-based persona

  • She is a woman who embraces her gentle heritage and stands firm in her boundaries.
  • She craves subtle affirmations, atmospheric beauty, emotional understanding, and cultural connection.
  • She scrolls to find pieces of herself in stories, art, and meaningful expressions.
  • Her energy is gentle yet impactful: she watches, senses, and recalls.
Content and publishing plan

What will be shared, where, and how often.

Content

Content

  • Content will be shared on Instagram, Pinterest.
  • The focus is on building a quiet, powerful digital presence rooted in Black femininity, emotional depth, and creative elegance.
  • Carousels feature soft truths and futuristic visuals under the recurring theme “What They Don’t Tell You…”.
  • AI-generated art and original text written in a spoken-word tone.
  • An AI-narrated short reel will be published once a week.
  • Reels pair a soft voiceover (via tools like ElevenLabs or CapCut’s AI voice generator).
  • Weekly mood boards or visual essays inspired by themes such as “Unseen Crowns,” “The Soft Weaponry of Silence,” or “Diasporic Joy.”

Voice & Tone

  • Expressive: capturing the essence of spoken word in writing.
  • Gentle Assurance: calm and confident.
  • Culturally Inspired: drawing from heritage, history, and future possibilities.

Publishing approach

  • Prioritise quality and emotional impact over posting frequency.
  • Keep the grid cohesive in colour and tone so the account feels like a visual journal.
  • Use captions to add context, not to explain everything.
